## 1. What is PDS Connect?

PDS Connect is a **business matching platform** designed to connect Buyers and Sellers at curated business events. As a Seller, you can:

- Browse Buyers registered for your event
- Receive match requests from Buyers who want to meet you
- Accept or decline those requests — and propose a meeting time
- Negotiate time slots through a simple back-and-forth process
- View and download your confirmed meeting schedule

> **Your role:** Sellers **respond** to match requests. Buyers reach out to you — you decide whether to accept and when to meet.

---

## 2. Logging In

1. Go to the PDS Connect portal (the link will be provided by your event organiser)
2. Enter your **email address** and **password**
3. Click **Sign In**

You will be taken directly to your Seller Dashboard.

> **Forgot your password?** Click "Forgot your password?" on the login page and a reset link will be sent to your email.

> **First time logging in?** Your account is created by the event admin. You should have received a welcome email with your login details. If not, contact your event organiser.

## 3. Your Dashboard

Once logged in, you'll land on your **Dashboard** — your home base on PDS Connect.

### What you'll see:

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| **Active Events** | Events currently open for matching and scheduling |
| **Archived Events** | Past or closed events (for reference only) |

### Event Cards

Each event card shows:
- Event name and dates
- Venue name

Click any of these buttons — or click the event card itself — to jump straight into that event.

## 4. Discovering Buyers

The **Discover** page lets you browse all Buyers participating in your event. This is a **read-only view** — you can research who's attending, but Buyers are the ones who initiate match requests.

### How to get there:
From your Dashboard → click your event → click **Discover** (or use the navigation bar at the top)

### What you'll see:

A grid of Buyer profile cards, each showing:
- Company logo / initials
- Company name
- Industry badge
- Tags (e.g. *Export-ready, FMCG, Government-linked*)
- Short bio

### Searching and Filtering

| Tool | How to use |
|---|---|
| **Search bar** | Type a company name or keyword from their bio |
| **Industry filter** | Select an industry from the dropdown |
| **Tag filter** | Select a specific tag |
| **Clear filters** | Resets everything back to showing all buyers |

### Viewing a Full Profile

Click on any Buyer card to open their **full profile** in a side panel. You'll see:
- Full company bio
- Website link
- Complete list of tags
- Industry

> **Tip:** Use this page to familiarise yourself with who's attending so you're prepared when their requests come in.

## 5. Your Inbox — Responding to Requests

Your **Inbox** is where all incoming match requests from Buyers appear. This is the most important page for Sellers.

### How to get there:
Dashboard → your event → **Inbox**

### What you'll see:

A list of Buyer cards, each showing:
- Buyer company name and logo
- Industry
- Tags
- Short bio

### Responding to a Request

For each request, you have two options:

---

#### Option A — Confirm Match

1. Click **Confirm Match**
2. A time slot selector will appear
3. Choose an available time slot from the dropdown
4. Click **Confirm**

The Buyer will be notified with your proposed meeting time. They can then accept, suggest a different time, or decline.

> **Note:** If no time slots are available in the dropdown, contact your event organiser — they may need to add more slots.

---

#### Option B — Decline

Click **Decline** if you do not wish to meet this Buyer. The request will be marked as Declined and removed from your Inbox.

---

> **Act promptly!** Requests may have a response deadline. If you don't respond in time, the system may auto-cancel the request.

## 6. Managing Your Matches

Your **Matches** page shows all your current and past match requests — both ones you've acted on and ones still in progress.

### How to get there:
Dashboard → your event → **Matches**

### Two Tabs:

**In Progress** — matches that are still active:
- Pending your response (in Inbox)
- Waiting for the Buyer to respond to your proposed time
- In active time negotiation

**Completed** — matches that have been resolved:
- Confirmed and scheduled meetings
- Declined requests
- Cancelled requests

### Actions Available Per Match

| Status | What it means | Your options |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | Buyer sent a request — not yet in your Inbox flow | — |
| **Awaiting Buyer** | You've proposed a time — waiting for Buyer | Wait for response |
| **Negotiating** | Buyer counter-proposed a time | Accept · Suggest New Time · Reject |
| **Scheduled** | Meeting confirmed ✓ | View in My Schedule |
| **Declined** | Request was declined | — |
| **Cancelled** | Request was cancelled | — |

## 7. Time Slot Negotiation

Once you confirm a match and propose a time, the Buyer may come back with a counter-proposal. Here's how the negotiation works:

### Step 1 — You Propose a Time
When confirming a match from your Inbox, you select an available time slot. This is your opening proposal to the Buyer.

### Step 2 — Buyer Responds
The Buyer can:
- **Accept** your time → Meeting is confirmed and added to both your schedules
- **Suggest New Time** → They counter-propose a different slot
- **Reject** → The match is declined

### Step 3 — You Respond to a Counter-Proposal

If the Buyer suggests a new time, you'll see the match appear in the **Negotiating** status on your Matches page, with a **countdown timer** showing how long you have to respond.

You then have three options:

**Accept** — You're happy with their proposed time. Meeting confirmed.

**Suggest New Time** — Their time doesn't work for you.
1. Click **Suggest New Time**
2. Select an available slot from the dropdown
3. Confirm — the Buyer will be notified

**Reject Meeting** — You no longer wish to proceed. The match will be marked as Declined.

### Countdown Timer Colours

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Normal | Plenty of time to respond |
| **Red** | Less than 6 hours remaining — respond soon! |
| **Expired** | Response window has closed |

### Back and Forth

The negotiation can go back and forth — you suggest a time, they counter, you counter again — until one party **accepts** or **rejects**. Each proposal has its own countdown timer.

## 8. Your Schedule

Once meetings are confirmed, they appear in your **My Schedule** page.

### How to get there:
Dashboard → your event → **My Schedule**

### What you'll see:

A table of all your **confirmed meetings**, grouped by date:

| Column | Details |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Meeting start and end time |
| **Buyer** | Company name and logo |
| **Venue** | Meeting location or room |

### Downloading Your Schedule

Click the **Download PDF** button at the top of the page to generate a clean, printable PDF of your schedule. The PDF includes:
- Event name and venue
- Each day's meetings in order
- Time, company name, and contact details

> **Tip:** Download your schedule the day before the event so you have it offline.

## 10. Tips & FAQs

**Q: How do I know when a new match request arrives?**
Check your **Inbox** regularly during the event's matching period. New requests will appear there as Buyers send them.

**Q: Do I have to meet every Buyer who requests me?**
No. You can Decline any request you receive. Only confirm the meetings that are valuable to you.

**Q: What if none of the available time slots work for me?**
After confirming, if the Buyer counter-proposes a time, you can accept their suggestion or counter back. If no slots work at all, contact your event organiser to discuss options.

**Q: What happens if I don't respond to a request in time?**
The system may automatically cancel the negotiation after the deadline. The Buyer will be freed to request other Sellers.

**Q: Can I update my company profile?**
Yes — click your name in the top navigation bar and select **Edit Profile**.

**Q: My confirmed meeting time no longer works — what do I do?**
If the status is **Negotiating**, use the **Suggest New Time** option. If the meeting is already **Scheduled**, contact your event organiser for assistance.

**Q: I can't log in — what should I do?**
Use the "Forgot your password?" link on the login page, or contact your event organiser for assistance.
