## 1. What is PDS Connect?

PDS Connect is a **business matching platform** designed to connect Buyers and Sellers at curated business events. As a Buyer, you can:

- Browse a curated list of Sellers participating in your event
- Send match requests to Sellers you'd like to meet
- Negotiate meeting times through a simple back-and-forth process
- View and download your confirmed meeting schedule

> **Your role:** Buyers **initiate** the matching process. You choose who you want to meet — Sellers then respond to your requests.

---

## 2. Logging In

1. Go to the PDS Connect portal (the link will be provided by your event organiser)
2. Enter your **email address** and **password**
3. Click **Sign In**

You will be taken directly to your Buyer Dashboard.

> **Forgot your password?** Click "Forgot your password?" on the login page and a reset link will be sent to your email.

> **First time logging in?** Your account is created by the event admin. You should have received a welcome email with your login details. If not, contact your event organiser.

## 3. Your Dashboard

Once logged in, you'll land on your **Dashboard** — your home base on PDS Connect.

### What you'll see:

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| **Active Events** | Events currently open for matching and scheduling |
| **Archived Events** | Past or closed events (for reference only) |

### Event Cards

Each event card shows:
- Event name and dates
- Venue name
- Quick-access buttons: **Discover**, **Matches**, **My Schedule**

Click any of these buttons — or click the event card itself — to jump straight into that event.

## 4. Discovering Sellers

The **Discover** page is where you browse all Sellers participating in your event.

### How to get there:
From your Dashboard → click your event → click **Discover** (or use the navigation bar at the top)

### What you'll see:

A grid of Seller profile cards, each showing:
- Company logo / initials
- Company name
- Industry badge
- Tags (e.g. *Export-ready, Halal, ISO9001*)
- Short bio

### Searching and Filtering

Use the tools at the top of the page to narrow down your results:

| Tool | How to use |
|---|---|
| **Search bar** | Type a company name or keyword from their bio |
| **Industry filter** | Select an industry from the dropdown |
| **Tag filter** | Select a specific tag |
| **Clear filters** | Resets everything back to showing all sellers |

### Viewing a Full Profile

Click on any Seller card to open their **full profile** in a side panel. You'll see:
- Full company bio
- Website link (click to open)
- Complete list of tags
- Industry

You can send a match request directly from this panel.

## 5. Requesting a Match

When you find a Seller you'd like to meet, click the **Request Match** button — either on their card or in their profile panel.

### What happens next:

1. Your request is sent to the Seller immediately
2. The button changes to **"Requested"** so you know it's been sent
3. The Seller will see your request in their Inbox
4. Once the Seller responds, you'll see the update in your **Matches** page

### Match Cap

Your event may have a **maximum number of match requests** you can send. Once you've reached this limit, the Request Match button will show **"Match Cap Reached"** and you won't be able to send more requests.

> **Tip:** Prioritise quality over quantity — focus on Sellers that are most relevant to your business goals.

## 6. Managing Your Matches

Your **Matches** page shows all your current and past match requests.

### How to get there:
Dashboard → your event → **Matches**

### Two Tabs:

**In Progress** — matches that are still active:
- Pending seller response
- Awaiting your decision on a proposed time
- In active time negotiation

**Completed** — matches that have been resolved:
- Confirmed and scheduled meetings
- Declined requests
- Cancelled requests

### Actions Available Per Match

The actions you can take depend on the current status of the match:

| Status | What it means | Your options |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for the Seller to respond | Cancel Request |
| **Awaiting Your Decision** | Seller has proposed a meeting time | Accept · Suggest New Time · Reject |
| **Negotiating** | You've counter-proposed a time | Wait for Seller's response |
| **Scheduled** | Meeting confirmed ✓ | View in My Schedule |
| **Declined** | Seller declined your request | — |
| **Cancelled** | Request was cancelled | — |

## 7. Time Slot Negotiation

After a Seller confirms your request, they'll propose a meeting time. Here's how the negotiation works:

### Step 1 — Seller Proposes a Time
You'll see the proposed time slot displayed on the match card, along with a **countdown timer** showing how long you have to respond.

> **Important:** If you don't respond before the timer runs out, the system may auto-cancel the negotiation. Respond promptly!

### Step 2 — You Decide

You have three options:

**Accept** — You're happy with the proposed time. The meeting is confirmed and will appear in your Schedule.

**Suggest New Time** — The proposed time doesn't work for you.
1. Click **Suggest New Time**
2. A dropdown will appear with available time slots
3. Select your preferred slot and confirm
4. The Seller will be notified and can then accept or counter-propose

**Reject Meeting** — You no longer wish to meet this Seller. The match will be marked as Declined.

### Countdown Timer Colours

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Normal | Plenty of time to respond |
| **Red** | Less than 6 hours remaining — respond soon! |
| **Expired** | Response window has closed |

### Back and Forth

The negotiation can go back and forth — you suggest a time, they counter, you counter again — until one party **accepts** or **rejects**. Each proposal has its own countdown timer.

## 8. Your Schedule

Once meetings are confirmed, they appear in your **My Schedule** page.

### How to get there:
Dashboard → your event → **My Schedule**

### What you'll see:

A table of all your **confirmed meetings**, grouped by date:

| Column | Details |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Meeting start and end time |
| **Seller** | Company name and logo |
| **Venue** | Meeting location or room |

### Downloading Your Schedule

Click the **Download PDF** button at the top of the page to generate a clean, printable PDF of your schedule. The PDF includes:
- Event name and venue
- Each day's meetings in order
- Time, company name, and contact details

> **Tip:** Download your schedule the day before the event so you have it offline.

## 10. Tips & FAQs

**Q: How many Sellers can I request to meet?**
Your event organiser sets a match cap. Once reached, you won't be able to send more requests. You can see how many you've used on the Discover page.

**Q: Can I cancel a match request after sending it?**
Yes — if the status is still **Pending**, you'll see a Cancel button on the match card.

**Q: What if the Seller doesn't respond in time?**
The system will automatically cancel the negotiation after the deadline. You can then browse and request other Sellers.

**Q: Can I update my profile?**
Yes — click your name in the top navigation bar and select **Edit Profile**.

**Q: My meeting time no longer works — what do I do?**
If the status is **Awaiting Your Decision** or **Negotiating**, use the **Suggest New Time** option to propose an alternative slot.

**Q: I can't log in — what should I do?**
Use the "Forgot your password?" link on the login page, or contact your event organiser for assistance.
